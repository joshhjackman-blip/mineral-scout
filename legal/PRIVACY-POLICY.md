## 1. Information we collect

### 1.1 Account and profile

- Email address, name, and authentication identifiers
- Organization / team membership and role
- Billing-related identifiers (Stripe customer IDs; we do not store full card numbers)
- Terms of Service signature records (typed name, email, IP address, user agent, timestamp, and version)

### 1.2 Platform usage

- Map interactions, tract and owner views, CRM notes and deal records you create, and similar workspace content
- Feature usage metrics (for example skip-trace counts) for billing, security, and product improvement
- Support tickets, demo requests, and other communications you send us

### 1.3 Platform dataset

The platform surfaces mineral ownership, parcel, well, permit, and related records compiled from public or licensed sources. That content is part of the product dataset. Your queries, views, and exports of it are associated with your account.

### 1.4 Skip-trace and contact enrichment

When you run a skip-trace through the platform, we may send owner identifiers (such as name and mailing address) to third-party data providers and receive phone numbers and email addresses in return. Results may be stored in a shared cache so that a later lookup for the same owner can be served without re-querying the provider. Cache entries are keyed on a normalized owner name and are not labeled with your company name.

You are responsible for complying with the Telephone Consumer Protection Act, Do-Not-Call rules, CAN-SPAM, and similar laws before calling, texting, or emailing any number or address.

### 1.5 Technical data

- IP address, browser user agent, and request metadata
- Cookies and similar technologies needed for authentication and session security
- Optional product analytics (for example PostHog) if configured

### 1.6 Payment data

Payments are processed by **Stripe**. Stripe collects and processes payment method details under its own privacy policy. We receive limited billing metadata (status and monthly skip-trace invoice totals).

## 2. How we use information

We use information to:

- provide, secure, and improve the platform;
- authenticate users and enforce access, billing, and these Terms;
- process skip-trace invoices;
- prevent abuse, fraud, and unauthorized access;
- respond to support and demo requests;
- comply with law and enforce our agreements; and
- understand aggregate product usage if analytics are enabled.

We do not sell personal information for money. We do not allow third parties to use platform contact data for their own independent marketing.

## 3. How we share information

We share information only as needed with:

- **Service providers** that host or operate the platform on our behalf (including database and authentication, application hosting, payments, transactional email, and skip-trace or enrichment vendors);
- **Other customers**, only indirectly via the shared skip-trace cache described above (enriched phones or emails for a normalized owner name, not your CRM notes or deal pipeline);
- **Professional advisers** or authorities when required by law or to protect rights, safety, and security; and
- a **successor** in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to this Policy or a successor policy with notice.

Team workspaces are isolated for CRM-style data (deals, notes, owner overrides). Platform dataset and shared skip-trace cache behavior are described above and in the Terms of Service.

## 4. Cookies and sessions

We use cookies and local storage primarily for authentication and session continuity. Without them, you cannot stay signed in. We do not use advertising cookies on the product application. If we enable product analytics, that tool may set its own cookies. You may request details at the privacy contact above.

## 5. Retention

We retain account, billing, signature, and workspace data for as long as your account is active and as needed for legitimate business, legal, and audit purposes. Skip-trace cache entries may persist so later lookups can avoid a repeat provider call. You may request deletion of your account data as described below; some records may be retained where we have a legal or contractual need to keep them.

## 6. Security

We use safeguards appropriate to a B2B software service, including encryption in transit, access-controlled databases, and authentication on product APIs. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

- access or correct account profile information;
